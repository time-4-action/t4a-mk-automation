Pricelist Sync — how it works

Copies product prices one way: T4A → CREAGLOBE. T4A is the single source of truth and is never written to. Only the price-list pairs configured in the admin portal (Automation → Pricelists) are ever touched.

A price list's code does NOT identify the same list in the other company. T4A 7 is "PP GOLD 2026" but CREAGLOBE 7 is "PP BRONZE 2026". Nine codes overlap between the two companies and every single pairing is wrong. Syncing by code would write GOLD prices into the BRONZE list — which is why nothing is synced unless a person has explicitly mapped the pair.

Finding the price lists

Metakocka has no endpoint that lists price lists. The only way to see them is json/product_list with return_pricelist: true: every product reports the lists it sits on, with the list's code, title, currency, validity and its price definition. The service scans the whole catalogue of each company and de-duplicates.

A price list carrying no products is invisible to this scan. A mapping whose target list was not seen is refused by default — put one product on it in Metakocka, or tick "allow unseen target" on that mapping.

A list's identity is the pair (sales_purchase, count_code), not the code alone: Metakocka numbers sales and purchase lists separately, so T4A code 1 is both "RRP 2025" (sales) and "Partner price 2026" (purchase).

What a run does

Products are matched by SKU (code), which lines up 1:1 between the two companies. For every mapped pair:

SituationWhat happens
Priced in T4A, missing from the CREAGLOBE listAdded with T4A's price
On both lists, a field T4A sets differsUpdated to match T4A
On both lists, already identicalLeft alone
On the CREAGLOBE list onlyLeft alone, counted as "extra"
Product not in CREAGLOBE at allSkipped — creating products is the Products sync's job

Fields are mirrored, not replaced: the payload is CREAGLOBE's current price definition overlaid with T4A's fields. T4A wins on everything it defines; a field only CREAGLOBE has (a discount T4A does not set, say) is carried through untouched. Change detection only looks at the fields T4A owns, so CREAGLOBE-only extras never cause pointless writes.

The max-change rail

Each mapping can carry a max change %. Any single price moving further than that is refused and reported instead of written.

This is not theoretical. On live data CREAGLOBE's "RRP 2025" held 2399 where T4A held 1966,39 — exactly a ×1.22 VAT factor, i.e. the two lists were not storing the same kind of number. Syncing that pair blind would have cut 1093 prices by 18%. Always run a Preview (dry run) before enabling a new pair.

Removals

Nothing is ever removed from a CREAGLOBE price list. Metakocka documents no reliable "remove product from a price list" call, and the product may legitimately be CREAGLOBE-only. Extra rows are counted per list so the drift stays visible, and cleaned up by hand in Metakocka if needed.
